# Code of conduct

We want VisualContract to be a respectful, useful, and accessible community. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, welcome differing levels of experience, and avoid harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's profile contact. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
